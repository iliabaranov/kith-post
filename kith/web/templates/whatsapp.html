What guests see, and what we can't do
What arrives
The card itself, as a picture from your own number, with a short message and their personal invitation link as the caption. The details and the RSVP live on that page, the same one email guests open. Nothing is added to the message to track them — but note the picture does pass through WhatsApp, the way any photo you send does.
Who it works for
People who already have your number, ideally in a chat you've spoken in before. Messaging lots of numbers that have never heard from you is exactly what WhatsApp restricts accounts for.
Reminders
The same schedule as email, in the same chat, and they stop the moment someone replies to the invitation.
If it stops working
WhatsApp sometimes ends a linked-device session. The card page will say so, and re-linking takes a scan or a typed code. Anything already sent keeps working, since the invitation lives on the web page, not in the message.
