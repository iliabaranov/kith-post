What guests see, and what we can't do
What arrives
A short plain text from your number — no picture, since a text can't carry one — with the guest's personal invitation link. The card, the details and the RSVP live on that page, the same one email guests open.
Reminders
The same schedule as email, by text, and they stop the moment someone replies to the invitation.
Replies
Land on the sending phone or in Twilio, as ordinary texts — this site never reads them. The one exception is a reply that is only the word STOP (or START), which is honoured and nothing more.
If it stops working
The card page says why, and this page shows the last error. For the phone route it is almost always the phone asleep or moved to a new address.
