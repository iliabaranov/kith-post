Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

{{ settings.app_name }} is a free, non-commercial, open-source digital-invitation service operated by Ilia Baranov as a personal project. It is self-hosted and designed to collect as little data as possible and to encrypt the personal data it does keep. This policy explains what we access, store, and do with your data — and how you can remove it.

Google user data we access

  • Basic profile & email (openid, email, profile) — your name, email address, and Google account ID, used solely to create and identify your account.
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send) — used only to send the invitations, cards, and reminders you create, from your own Gmail account. We cannot read, search, modify, or delete anything in your mailbox; the send-only scope does not grant that access.

What we store, and how we protect it

  • Your account name, email, and a Google OAuth refresh token (so the app can send on your behalf) — encrypted at rest.
  • The cards/events you create, your recipient lists and their RSVPs, and your address-book contacts — recipient and contact names, email addresses {%- if settings.whatsapp_configured %} and phone numbers{% endif %} are encrypted at rest.
    • {% if settings.whatsapp_configured %}
  • If you link WhatsApp (see below): the fact that you have, the connection's last known status, and your own WhatsApp number — encrypted at rest. Your WhatsApp login itself is never stored by {{ settings.app_name }}; it stays in the WhatsApp service's own storage on this server.
    • {% endif %}
  • Card images you upload — the heavy full-resolution copy is automatically purged after the event's retention window.

Data is encrypted at rest (Fernet) and served over HTTPS in transit. The service runs as a single self-hosted container; there are no third-party analytics, ads, or trackers.

How we use it

To authenticate you, send the messages you compose — from your Gmail{% if settings.whatsapp_configured %}, or from your WhatsApp if you have linked it{% endif %} — show you honest delivery/RSVP status, and send the follow-up reminders you enable. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. The only outbound flow is the message you choose to send, to the recipients you specify — by email through Google's Gmail API from your own account{% if settings.whatsapp_configured %}, or, if you have linked it, through WhatsApp from your own account{% endif %}.

{% if settings.whatsapp_configured %}

WhatsApp (optional)

You may optionally link your own WhatsApp account so invitations and reminders can be sent through it instead of by email. This is entirely opt-in: nothing is connected until you accept a warning explaining the risks, and you can unlink at any time.

  • Messages are sent from your own account, by a WhatsApp service running on this same server. Message content, the card image and recipient numbers go to WhatsApp (Meta) as they would if you sent the photo yourself, and are then subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. (Cards sent by email never leave this server — they're attached to the message.)
  • A WhatsApp message contains a short line of text and the recipient's invitation link — nothing is added to track them. As with email, the only signals we record are the ones the invitation page sees.
  • This uses an unofficial WhatsApp client, which is against WhatsApp's terms of service and can get an account restricted or banned. That risk is yours, and is why the feature is opt-in and clearly warned.
  • Unlinking, and deleting your account, remove the stored WhatsApp connection from this server.
{% endif %}

Limited Use

{{ settings.app_name }}'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention & deletion

  • Your account page offers a one-click export and a permanent delete that removes your account and cascades to your events, recipients, and contacts.
  • Full-resolution images are auto-purged after the retention window.
  • You can revoke {{ settings.app_name }}'s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Recipients of your invitations

Recipients get no account and no cookies, and there is no tracking pixel. "Opened" simply means a person visited the invitation page — we exclude the automatic fetches that chat apps and search engines make to build link previews, so it counts readers rather than robots. Their email and name are stored encrypted so the invitation can be delivered and RSVPs recorded.

Cookies

One signed session cookie keeps you logged in. No third-party or advertising cookies are used.

Children

{{ settings.app_name }} is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.
