What you're signing up for
Can {{ settings.app_name }} read my email?
No. You give permission to send a message on your behalf — nothing else. {{ settings.app_name }} can't read, search, or delete anything in your inbox (the Google permission is "send only").
Who actually sends the invites?
You do. They go out from your own Gmail and land in your Sent folder, so they arrive as a personal note from you — not a marketing blast.
What do you store about me?
Your name and email, the cards you make, and the people you invite — and the personal bits are encrypted. No tracking pixels, no ads, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.
What about the people I invite?
They get no account, no cookies, and no tracking pixel. "Opened" simply means someone visited the invitation page.
Can I get my data out — or delete everything?
Anytime. Your account page has a one-click export and a permanent delete.
Is it really free?
Yes — a free, open-source project, self-hosted by a friend. No ads, no fees, no catch.
